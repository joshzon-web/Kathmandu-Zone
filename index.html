Family recipes from the Kathmandu valley

Kathmandu Zone isn’t just about food; it’s an immersive experience. With vibrant branches in Hounslow and Southall, Kathmandu Zone invites you to relish the authentic flavours and warm hospitality of Nepal.

Proudly serving creative Nepalese cuisine using unique family recipes, we aim to provide a traditional and authentic dining experience. Our goal is to offer the best possible experience, expanding the horizons of your palate.

Our chefs have honed their skills to deliver an exceptional fusion of Nepalese cuisine. The Kathmandu Zone team looks forward to welcoming you.

Newari dishes and the tandoor

Our Newari menu covers the small plates of Kathmandu’s Newar community: choila, sekuwa, sukuti, chatpate and more. The Newari Khaja Set brings a selection of them together on one plate.

Alongside these you’ll find tandoori grills, sizzlers, noodles and Himalayan curries.
